ANALYTICS METHODOLOGY

How the numbers work.

A plain-language guide to the original command centre's ideas, adapted for confirmed official PUBG data.

Metric definitions

Kills per match
Reported lifetime or mode kills divided by rounds played. Not PUBG K/D, because deaths can differ from matches.
Damage per match
Official saved damage dealt divided by saved rounds in the same scope.
Win and Top 10 rate
Reported wins or Top 10 finishes divided by rounds played. These depend on the game mode.
Roles
Exploratory Fragger, Support, Survivor or Hybrid classification relative to the current verified clan. Insufficient data is shown until enough real evidence exists.
Unofficial rating
A weighted 0–100 within-clan percentile model. Only sufficiently observed profiles are rated. This is not PUBG's official ranked rating or a statistical confidence interval.
Real historical change
Two actual observations dated 6–14 days apart. Counter resets and missing baseline dates do not become imagined improvements or declines.
Source-provided bot exposure
An unverified field in a local CSV file; not an official PUBG API measure here. It never changes the live player rating, clan roster or awards.
CSV imports
Local preview only. The official Supabase database is untouched. A future manager-only persistent import would need authenticated authorisation and a separate source label.
Sample size
Large, Medium and Limited labels describe observed match counts. They are not a confidence level or probability of accuracy.
