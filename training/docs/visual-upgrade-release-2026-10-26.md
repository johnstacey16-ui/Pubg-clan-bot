# HELP Clan visual upgrade — release 2026-10-26

**Status:** released to `main` after the repository owner requested the merge. See [merged PR #41](https://github.com/johnstacey16-ui/HELP-Clan-PUBG-Toolkit/pull/41) and merge commit `d54ac7cb4ba83113a54f9a6a989581c4340b7aed`.

**Live site:** https://help-clan-pubg-toolkit.vercel.app/

The eight-tool visual upgrade includes original, non-official representative illustrations for 45 named weapons, seven magnified optic references and distinct sight artwork, selected attachment categories, backpacks and equipment. Damage, sensitivity and other numerical logic remain unchanged; the Model 2 portrait and 13 hit zones stay approved and unchanged.

**The important mobile fix:** Combat DB weapon lists remain text-and-numbers only. Illustrations are shown **only when a weapon's detail panel is opened**, never as unsolicited row thumbnails or automatic gun cards. Range/TTK/Recoil remain statistics-first, with illustrative imagery in closed-by-default expanders; those media files are not downloaded until requested.

**Verified:** screenshot-based readability correction fixed the initially dim Scope Matcher; 16 phone/desktop screenshot captures in [Actions #40281996396](https://github.com/johnstacey16-ui/HELP-Clan-PUBG-Toolkit/actions/runs/40281996396); successful full post-merge [GitHub Actions #40282223490](https://github.com/johnstacey16-ui/HELP-Clan-PUBG-Toolkit/actions/runs/40282223490); Vercel production READY and live alias verified against the exact merge SHA.

**Not represented as completed:** owner testing on a physical Xbox Series S in Edge and physical Android/desktop, on-device performance/network/LCP/CLS measurements, assistive technology review and optional source-cleared photo-grade artwork.
